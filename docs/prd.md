# CarbonCanvas - Product Requirements Document (PRD)

## Overview

CarbonCanvas is a Carbon Footprint Awareness Platform designed to help individuals understand, track, and reduce their environmental impact through visual storytelling, personalized insights, and behavioral nudges.

Unlike traditional carbon calculators that only display numbers and charts, CarbonCanvas transforms carbon footprint data into a living digital ecosystem that reflects the user's environmental choices.

The goal is to create awareness rather than simply provide tracking metrics.

---

## Problem Statement

Many individuals are unaware of the environmental impact of their daily activities.

Existing carbon tracking solutions often present data in a numerical format that fails to create emotional engagement or lasting behavioral change.

Users need a simple and engaging way to understand how their choices affect the environment and what actions they can take to improve.

---

## Vision

To transform carbon footprint tracking into an interactive awareness experience that encourages sustainable decision-making through visualization, personalization, and actionable insights.

## Core Features

### 1. Daily Activity Logging

Users can log activities related to:

* Transportation
* Food consumption
* Energy usage
* Shopping habits

---

### 2. Carbon Footprint Calculation

The system estimates a daily carbon footprint score using predefined emission factors.

Output includes:

* Daily footprint
* Weekly trend
* Monthly trend

---

### 3. CarbonCanvas Ecosystem

The application visualizes environmental impact through a dynamic ecosystem.

Environmental dimensions:

* Forest Health
* Water Quality
* Air Quality
* Biodiversity

Positive habits improve ecosystem health.

Negative habits degrade ecosystem health.

---

### 4. Personalized AI Insights

The AI engine analyzes:

* Activity patterns
* Carbon trends
* Ecosystem state

Generates:

* Personalized observations
* Improvement opportunities
* Actionable recommendations

---

### 5. Impact Simulator

Users can experiment with behavioral changes.

Examples:

* What if I cycle instead of driving?
* What if I reduce meat consumption?

The platform predicts environmental improvements before users make changes.

---

### 6. Progress Dashboard

Displays:

* Carbon trends
* Ecosystem evolution
* Sustainability streaks
* Personal achievements
